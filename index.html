    Ziyu Wang, Chenyuan Liu, Yushun Xiang, Runhao Zhang, Qingbo Hao, Hongliang Lu, Houyu Chen, Zhizhong Feng, Kaiyue Zheng, Dehao Ye, Xianchao Zeng, Xinyu Zhou, Boran Wen, Jiaxin Li, Mingyu Zhang, Kecheng Zheng, Qian Zhu, Ran Cheng, Yong-Lu Li

  3. ICML 2026

    SpecPrune-VLA: Accelerating Vision-Language-Action Models via Action-Aware Self-Speculative Pruning

    Hanzhen Wang, Jiaming Xu, Yushun Xiang, Jiayi Pan, Yongkang Zhou, Yong-Lu Li, Guohao Dai

    International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 2026

    A training-free, two-level token pruning scheme for VLA policies: action-level static pruning plus layer-level dynamic pruning, steered by an action-aware controller, for faster inference at near-identical success rates.
